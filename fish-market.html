Fish Market

Contemporary interior design that blends warmth, texture, and volume.

Concept

Situated along the Miami River, this contemporary fish market unfolds beneath a continuous, undulating roof that gathers three connected volumes — market, circulation, and restaurant — under a single fluid gesture evoking the movement of water. Glass facades dissolve the boundary between inside and out, opening the market hall and restaurant terrace directly onto curved pathways, organic planters, and green plazas that thread between the volumes. Circulation becomes a public connection, drawing visitors from the market through landscaped space toward the restaurant's exterior terrace, where the building's edge softens into park. Rather than enclosing commerce and gastronomy separately, the design lets architecture and landscape extend into one another, tying the market back to its riverfront setting. The market hall accommodates 20 vendor stalls along 10 ft aisles, supported by a covered loading dock 2,100 Sq Ft, a dedicated cleaning and processing area 1,300 Sq Ft, and cold storage for ice and vendor supplies 1,000 Sq Ft.
