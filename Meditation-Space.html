Concept

A small space for presence and reflection

This meditation space, built entirely of wood, takes the form of two open, interlocking curves formed by dense rows of timber posts, wrapping around existing trees rather than displacing them. Left without a roof, the structure lets the tree canopy above become its natural cover, blending the built form directly into the landscape. Its open sides allow light, air, and movement to pass freely through, offering a quiet retreat carved out of calm and reflection.
